This Privacy Policy outlines how I handle information when you visit my site. **TLDR: I do not track you, I do not use cookies, and I do not collect your personal information.**

1. Analytics & Data Collection
* To understand how my website is performing and to improve the content I create, I use Cabin (withcabin.com), a privacy-first web analytics service.
* Because I highly value your privacy, my analytics setup is intentionally minimal. When you visit marksuth.dev, Cabin collects anonymous, aggregated data. Cabin does not:

* Use cookies or local storage to track you.
* Use device fingerprinting.
* Track you across other websites or devices.
* Collect or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your IP address.

What I do see:
* I only have access to high-level, aggregate data about my website traffic. This includes information such as:
* Total page views and unique visitors.
* Top referring websites (how you found my site).
* General device types (e.g., mobile vs. desktop) and browser usage.
* Country-level location data.
* This information is used strictly to understand website performance so I can continue to improve the user experience.

2. Cookies
* I do not use cookies on marksuth.dev. Because I use Cabin for analytics instead of traditional tracking tools like Google Analytics, there are no cookie banners on this site, and no tracking scripts are placed on your device.

3. Personal Information You Provide
* If you choose to contact me directly via email or social media, I will only use the personal information you provide (such as your name and email address) to respond to your inquiry. I will never sell, rent, or share your contact information with third parties.

4. Third-Party Links
* My website may contain links to other websites or resources. Please note that I have no control over the privacy practices or content of those third-party sites. I encourage you to read the privacy policies of any website you visit.

5. Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
* Under various global privacy laws (including GDPR, CCPA, and PECR), you have rights regarding your personal data. Because my website operates entirely without collecting or storing any personally identifiable information, there is no personal data of yours for me to access, correct, or delete.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
* I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in my practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date" at the top.
